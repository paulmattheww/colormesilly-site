Cancel ColorMeSilly! Plus

Plus is available as a monthly plan (7-day free trial, then $4.99/month) or a yearly plan ($34.99/year). Both auto-renew through Apple. To cancel either plan:

  1. Open the iOS Settings app
  2. Tap your Apple ID name
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap ColorMeSilly! Plus
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

You can also manage subscriptions on the web at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Credits

Optional credit packs are $2.99 for 25, purchased through Apple. Credits are not a subscription and do not auto-renew.

Photos & privacy

Photos leave the device only after a grown-up math check, and only to create line art. Read the full Privacy Policy.
