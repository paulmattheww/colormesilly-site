Privacy Policy

This policy is for ColorMeSilly! (bundle ID com.colormesilly.app), published by Sovereign Action LLC.

Who we are

Sovereign Action LLC operates ColorMeSilly!. Contact us at paul.washburn@sovereign-action.com.

Not a Kids Category app

ColorMeSilly! is not in the App Store Kids category. A grown-up math check is required before any photo leaves the device.

What we do not collect

ColorMeSilly! has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no tracking. We do not create user profiles or sell personal information.

Photo & Video

The app uses Photo & Video access so you can choose a photo to turn into coloring-page line art. A photo leaves your device only after a grown-up math check. It is then sent to fal.ai (FLUX.1 Kontext) over TLS for line-art generation. We do not store the photo.

Who processes a photo

fal.ai processes the photo solely to generate line art. The transfer uses TLS. We do not keep a copy of the photo on our servers.

Purchases

ColorMeSilly! Plus and credit packs are sold through Apple. Apple processes payment information under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

  • Plus: 7-day free trial, then $4.99 per month, auto-renewing through Apple
  • Credits: $2.99 for 25

Data we do not keep

Because we do not store the photo and do not run accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, there is no ColorMeSilly! account or photo archive to delete on our side. To stop future photo processing, do not send photos through the app. To stop Plus billing, cancel in iOS Settings (see Support).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
