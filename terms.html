What the app does

ColorMeSilly! turns a photo you choose into thick-outline coloring-page line art you can color, save, share, or print. A grown-up math check is required before any photo leaves your device and before purchases.

Not for the Kids Category

ColorMeSilly! is not in the App Store Kids category. It is meant for families with a grown-up present for photo upload and purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as an account or profile.

Photos and third-party processing

After the grown-up check, a selected photo is sent to fal.ai over TLS solely to generate line art. We do not store your photo on our servers. You are responsible for only uploading photos you have the right to use.
